# Non-Euclidean Statistics Notes

**A collection of learning notes, research insights, and paper reviews on statistics beyond Euclidean spaces.**

This repository documents my ongoing exploration of **Non-Euclidean Statistics**, with a focus on both fundamental concepts and recent advances in statistical theory and methodology.

The goal is to build a systematic understanding of statistical methods for complex data objects, including manifold-valued data, probability distributions, graphs, trees, and other non-Euclidean structures.

Each issue combines two complementary perspectives:

- **Foundations:** Mathematical concepts, statistical principles, key definitions, and illustrative examples.
- **Research Frontiers:** Recent papers, emerging methodologies, theoretical developments, and potential research directions.

### Topics of Interest

- Manifold and Riemannian Statistics
- Metric Space Statistics and Fréchet Regression
- Optimal Transport and Wasserstein Statistics
- Hyperbolic and Geometric Statistics
- Statistical Inference for Complex Data Objects
- Robust Estimation and Asymptotic Theory
- Graph, Network, and Shape Statistics

### Philosophy

**Learning the foundations while exploring the frontiers.**

Rather than separating textbook learning from current research, this repository aims to connect mathematical foundations with modern statistical problems, theoretical insights, and methodological innovations.

Notes are primarily written in Chinese, with mathematical notation and technical terminology in English.
